Prompt: How can Time Molecules help enterprises understand how processes affect other processes as a core business capability, rather than merely optimizing one workflow at a time? Think in a novel, modern, post-AI business setting.

Abstract: Time Molecules is not mainly about optimizing isolated workflows. Its deeper enterprise application is cross-process intelligence: modeling how processes, event sequences, and story fragments affect each other across departments, systems, and time. Traditional BI integrated facts across domains so organizations could compare shared measures. Time Molecules extends that idea to the time side of the enterprise by integrating event sequences, linked cases, case and event properties, Markov models, Bayesian-style conditional probabilities, timing relationships, model similarities, and signal models. This allows an enterprise to see how one process changes the boundary conditions of another, such as how promotions affect fulfillment and returns, how ER triage affects lab, imaging, discharge, and bed pressure, or how AI-agent actions alter human escalation work and downstream risk. The core capability is a governed model layer for process interaction intelligence: understanding how local changes propagate through the larger business ecology.
