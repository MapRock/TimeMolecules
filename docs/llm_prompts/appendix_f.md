Prompt: How do Non-Deterministic Finite Automata (NFAs) complement Markov models as a “time crystal” in Time Molecules?

Abstract: Markov models serve as a probabilistic first draft of a process, while Non-Deterministic Finite Automata (NFAs) crystallize high-confidence patterns into precise, rule-based recognizers that run in real time. NFAs maintain multiple active states in parallel and can recognize exact sequences (or variants) instantly, even with nondeterministic branching. Together they form a powerful pair: Markov models hypothesize likely transitions; NFAs enforce the refined rules discovered from those hypotheses.
