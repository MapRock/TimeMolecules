Prompt: What is the difference between cause/action and event/state in Time Molecules?

Abstract: Cause/action represents the intentional trigger (“why” something happens), while event/state captures the resulting condition or change (“what happened”). In the Insight Space Graph and Tuple Correlation Web, these are modeled separately yet linked through sequencing, conditional probabilities, and clustering. Clear separation enables the system to move from raw correlations toward true causal understanding and actionable intelligence.
