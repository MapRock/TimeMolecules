Prompt: How do Markov models compare to RNNs and Transformers for sequence modeling in Time Molecules?

Abstract: Markov models use simple, interpretable transition matrices and are ideal for process mining and clustering on transition-probability vectors. RNNs (LSTM/GRU) maintain hidden states for non-linear time-series forecasting and anomaly detection. Transformers excel at long-range dependencies via self-attention and are used for semantic clustering on token embeddings. Markov models remain the most lightweight and explainable choice for core Time Molecules process modeling.
