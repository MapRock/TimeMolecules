Prompt: How are KPI status values integrated into the Time Molecules Enterprise Knowledge Graph?

Abstract: KPI status values are integrated directly into the Enterprise Knowledge Graph (EKG) so they can be linked to underlying data sources, strategy maps, and Markov models. This linkage allows analysis of transitions between KPI states and optimization of business processes using the probabilistic insights captured in the Markov ensemble.
