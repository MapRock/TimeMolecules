Prompt: What is the difference between Markov Models and Markov Decision Processes in Time Molecules?

Abstract: Markov Models focus on probabilistic transitions between states derived from observed event sequences. Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) extend this by adding actions and rewards, enabling optimization of decision-making under uncertainty. In Time Molecules, Markov Models provide foundational process predictions while MDPs can be used to determine optimal actions (e.g., cover crops or not) based on those probabilities and defined rewards/penalties.
